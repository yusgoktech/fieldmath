Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your inputs, settings and saved calculations stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings (language, units, display options, theme), the last values entered in each tool, your saved calculations, the free uses of Pro tools you have used, and a few local counters that decide when the system rating prompt may be shown. This data stays on your device and we do not receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

PDF files are created on your device and are shared only where you choose to send them.

Purchases

The optional one-time FieldMath Pro purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

To verify purchases and restore them on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase history (product, date, store receipt) and basic device and app information needed for verification. We do not link this data to your name or e-mail, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Ratings

The app may ask the operating system to show its standard rating prompt. Apple or Google decide whether it is shown, and any rating you give is handled by them.

Children

FieldMath is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For purchase records you can contact Apple or Google. You can also ask us to request deletion of the anonymous RevenueCat record of your app installation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
